Write one funny line for the One-Line Laugh-Off: a single joke that lands on a cold read, with no setup notes and no explanation, at most 200 characters. Aim for a hard, sudden laugh, a twist the reader did not see coming, every word earning its place, and a fresh idea rather than a recycled setup. My approach: collide two frames — a calendar inviting me to an event, and my own funeral — then let the punchline turn on the double meaning of "committing" (RSVP-ing vs committing to things), so the narrator dodges even his own funeral. The line: "My calendar invited me to my own funeral, so I RSVP'd 'maybe' — I still hate committing to things."
